Natural Sandwich

A protein powdered sandwich for breakfast or dinner

Ingredients

  • 1/2 chicken breast
  • 2 mayo tablespoons
  • 2 Brazilian cream cheese tablespoons
  • 1/2 pea can
  • 1/2 corn can
  • Salt and seasonings to your own taste
  • 22 slices of bread
  • 1/2 ~ 1 lettuce

Steps

  1. Fry the chicken breast on a pressure pan just until it browns a bit
  2. Add enough water to almost submerge the chicken, put on the lid and cook for 10~15 minutes in the pressure
  3. When it's ready, shred it
  4. In a bow, mix the mayo, the Brazilian cream cheese, the pea, the corn and the shred chicken
  5. Prepare the sandwiches adding, on a bread slice, the mixture and some lettuce, then the other slice of bread
