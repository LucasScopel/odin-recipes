Grounded Beef Pie

A simple and fast idea to add some pie into your routine

Ingredients

  • 300g of grounded beef
  • 1 onion
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1/2 carrot
  • 1/2 can of pea
  • 1/2 can of corn
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons of Brazilian granulated tapioca
  • 1/2 teaspoon of baking powder
  • Salt and seasonings to your own taste

Steps

  1. Chop the onion and the garlic and then fry them in a saucepan
  2. Grate the carrot and add to the same saucepan
  3. Add the meat and cook until almost ready, then add the corn and the pea
  4. Mix, in a bow, the eggs, the Brazilian granulated tapioca and the baking powder
  5. Add the mixture and the meat to 8 small ramekins and mix them
  6. Bake for 30 to 40 minutes at 180°C
