Salami Pizza Sandwich

Text will be displayed here

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of bred
  • 4 slices of salami
  • 3 tablespoons of tomato sausage
  • 1 slice of mozzarella
  • Oregano to your own taste

Steps

  1. On a frying pan, fry the salami
  2. On a bread's slice, add the tomato sausage, the cheese, the fried salami and then the oregano
  3. Finish it by toasting a bit on the same frying pan
